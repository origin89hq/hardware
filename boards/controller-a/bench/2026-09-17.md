# Board A, revision A: a night with a real RS-485 slave, 2026-09-16 to 2026-09-17

The second unattended night, and the first with something other than the board
itself on a bus: a Waveshare Modbus RTU IO 8CH on channel 2 and a 12 V pilot
lamp on its output 8. 16 h 48 min from 15:16 to 08:05, with the laptop and the
debug probe disconnected for all of it.

## What was connected

- A bench supply at 14.4 V, limit raised to 1 A after the previous afternoon's
  brown-outs, with the PZEM-003/017 meter now in the **common negative at the
  supply** rather than in board A's branch, so it measures everything.
- The Modbus RTU IO 8CH on CN3: pin 1 to `485 A+`, pin 2 to `485 B-`, pin 3 to
  its `DGND`, powered from the same supply, at its factory address 1 and
  9600 8N1. JP2 fitted.
- A 12 V pilot lamp between `DO COM` and `DO8`, with `DO COM` fed from the
  supply positive, as these sinking outputs require.
- Three DS18B20 probes on the 1-Wire bus, CAN in external loopback, VE.Direct
  open, no board B, selector reading off.
- Channel 1 alone on the old loopback wiring, so the RS-485 pair test stands
  down by design.

Image `2a6ae2a7`: `o89-selftest` with `esp-stress`, `pzem` and `modbus-io`,
built at `DEFMT_LOG=debug` so the firmware's debug lines are in it. The board
was fully reset at 15:13, ran its watchdog proof, and came back proven.

## What I was trying to find out

Whether the counters record survives a night now that the supply cannot
brown the board out, after both FRAM slots were lost the previous afternoon.
And whether a real Modbus slave survives being polled once a second for a
night, which no bus on this board had ever been asked to do: every RS-485
result before this was the board talking to itself.

## What happened

One run, 16 h 48 min, ended only by the ST-Link being plugged back in, which
restarts the board as a software reset. The reset history reads watchdog 1 and
software 2: the proof at 15:13, the flash at 15:16, and this morning's
reconnect. **No power resets.** The counters record came through intact.

## What surprised me

Only one failure in 58 168 polls, and it was not a silence: code 11 at 02:48,
a reply that would not parse. A single garbled frame in a night is a believable
rate for a short unterminated-at-one-end bench lead, but the cause is not
known, and one event is not a pattern. It is recorded here so that a second one
has something to be compared against.

The supply held to 0.05 V across the night while the board's own input sagged
0.38 V between 01:15 and 03:06. Both readings move together, so that is the
divider's 1 % offset moving with temperature rather than a supply problem: the
MCU's own low of 26.6 °C is at 01:15, the same minute as the house low.

## What became a test

The `modbus-io` build reads the module's eight inputs and eight outputs every
second and toggles output 8 every thirty polls, reading the coils back to
confirm the write took. It counts into the RS-485 counter with its own codes:
10 no reply, 11 unparseable, 12 exception, 13 output did not read back.

Two guards came out of the bring-up. The first three polls of a boot are not
counted, because board A asks within a second of power-up while the module is
still starting, and one silence there used to hold the fault lamp on for a
whole run while saying nothing about the bus. The second is that the loopback
conductor stands down when fewer than two channels are left to it: over an
empty set of listeners its all-heard check returns true, so it would have
recorded passes for a test nobody ran.

## Still open

- What the garbled reply at 02:48 was. The NOR event ring holds it, but
  o89-dev cannot read the ring: only the boot dump or the both-selector-inputs-
  low trick will show it. Reading the ring over SWD is worth adding.
- Whether one brown-out can invalidate both counter slots, carried over from
  [2026-09-16](2026-09-16.md).
- Board B's interlock, the selector switch, VE.Direct with a real device, and
  RS-485 on a long cable with idle bias, all still untested.
- The module's behaviour on power loss: Waveshare documents no communication
  watchdog and no configurable power-on output state, so what DO8 does when the
  module is power-cycled with the output on has not been established. That
  matters before anything but a lamp hangs off it.
